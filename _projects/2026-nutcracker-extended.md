We're investigating bending stress in the beams, so I analyzed the nutcracker I created, found its elastic equation, then plugged in 2% of its length for its maximum deflection, then found a corresponding beam type that would allow my maximum deflection to be below that threshold. The right hand side shows my finished product with shown beam cross section.
